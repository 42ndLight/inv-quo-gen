# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Product Overview**

A lightweight, visually stunning web application for generating, managing, and exporting professional PDF quotations and invoices tailored for equipment hire and service businesses.

**Target User**

Machinery hire companies, contractors, and service providers needing quick line-item quote generation with dynamic rate calculations and dual-signoff capabilities.

**Key Features**

* **Instant Dynamic Form**: Reactive line-item addition with real-time rate and total calculation (`Hours/Unit * Rate = Amount`).
* **Document Lifecycle**: One-click conversion of pending Quotations (`DK/QUO/...`) to finalized Invoices (`DK/INV/...`).
* **Custom Branding & Signatures**: Customizable vendor headers, client attention fields, and dual signature stamp blocks for official sign-offs.
* **Pixel-Perfect PDF Generation**: Export visually polished PDFs containing full layout parity with company footers, references, and totals.
